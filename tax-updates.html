ITR and audit report due dates extended for specified AY 2026-27 cases

CBDT Circular No. 07/2026 extends the specified ITR due date from 31 October 2026 to 21 November 2026 and the corresponding audit-report due date from 30 September 2026 to 21 October 2026.

Read 10-line professional summary
  1. CBDT issued Circular No. 07/2026 dated 28 September 2026.
  2. The circular concerns specified taxpayers for AY 2026-27.
  3. The applicable ITR due date is extended from 31 October 2026.
  4. The revised ITR filing deadline is 21 November 2026.
  5. The corresponding tax audit report deadline is also extended.
  6. The audit report due date moves from 30 September 2026 to 21 October 2026.
  7. Taxpayers should confirm that they fall within the specified category.
  8. Audit work, reconciliations and tax computations should not be deferred.
  9. Teams should separately track the audit-report and ITR deadlines.
  10. Action: verify the circular and plan filing well before the revised dates.
